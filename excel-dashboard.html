Harborline dashboard

An interactive workbook you open in Microsoft Excel and click through. Harborline is fictional. The book is won revenue for January through October, 2025 and 2026. Slicers filter the scorecards, three charts, and the detail table together.

Open the workbook Read the number write-up

How to walk through it

Open the file in the Excel desktop app. Start on Dashboard. The Walkthrough sheet has the ten steps and the numbers you should see. Use one slicer at a time, then clear it. The clear-filter icon is on the slicer header. Leave Year on 2026 unless a step says otherwise.

  • Scorecards: revenue, active seats, conversion, and year-over-year growth, each with a chip against 2025.
  • Quarter slicer: the line chart keeps only the months in that quarter, and growth restates against the same quarter last year.
  • Region, product line, and status: the rank chart, the stacked mix, and the table move with the same filters.
  • Status = Won hides losses, so conversion becomes 100 percent. Clear it to put the rate back.

What 2026 shows before you filter

  • Won revenue is $19.7 million, up 7.2 percent ($1.3 million). Active seats are 19,496, up 15.4 percent.
  • Conversion is 80.2 percent, down 4.3 points from 2025.
  • Cloud Seats rise through the year. Hardware Refresh slopes down, about 18 percent below 2025. Security Add-ons are up 43.2 percent.
  • Training jumps from about $79 thousand in February to about $209 thousand in March, then again in September.
  • Pacific South is the top territory, at $2.9 million. Mountain grows only 1.2 percent. Its mix is heavy on hardware.
  • South conversion falls to 66.7 percent, from 84.5 percent in 2025. Revenue there still grows. The win rate is the problem.

How it is built

  • Data is an Excel table named Bookings: one row per quote, with region, territory, product line, segment, and status.
  • Revenue on the cards is won quotes only. Lost and open quotes stay in the table at $0 so they do not inflate the total.
  • PivotTables feed the line chart, the territory bars, the stacked mix, and the detail grid. Slicers are connected to those pivots.
  • The Year slicer is not connected to the comparison pivot, so growth can use 2025 and 2026 at the same time. Quarter, region, product line, and status apply to both years.
  • 2025 has no 2024 baseline. Selecting that year leaves the dollars in place and the chips say so.

The generator is build_dashboard.py. Notes are in the folder readme.
