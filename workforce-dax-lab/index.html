NovaTech skills table from a course scenario. The measure counts employees who still need reskilling and leaves the company total row out.

Measure

Calculated for the current filter. The total row stays out.

Sum that includes the stored total row

1,221

This is the mistake the measure is written to avoid.

Department Total HC Employees needing reskilling

Measure

Employees Needing Reskilling =
CALCULATE (
    SUM ( '4_Skills_Inventory'[Employees Needing Reskilling] ),
    '4_Skills_Inventory'[Department] <> "COMPANY TOTAL"
)

A measure runs again for the filter on the page. The numbers in the table are already stored on each row. With no filter the measure is 1,221. Sales is 173. Adding the COMPANY TOTAL row counts those employees twice and shows 2,442.
