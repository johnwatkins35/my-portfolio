You can use normal PowerShell here: commands, help, files, tables, scripts, and modules.

Find a command
  Get-Command
  Get-Command *Csv*
  Get-Help Get-ChildItem -Examples

Files in /work
  Get-ChildItem
  Get-Content ./cheatsheet.txt
  Set-Content ./note.txt 'hello'
  Add-Content ./note.txt 'second line'
  Copy-Item ./note.txt ./note-copy.txt
  Remove-Item ./note.txt

Tables
  Get-Content ./tables.txt

Data
  Import-Csv ./services.csv | Format-Table
  Import-Csv ./services.csv | ConvertTo-Json
  Import-Csv ./services.csv | Where-Object Status -eq Running | Sort-Object Name
  Import-Csv ./services.csv | Group-Object Status | Select-Object Name, Count

Scripts
  pwsh -File ./yourscript.ps1

Modules, saved for the next time you open PSLab
  Install-Module Pester -Scope CurrentUser -Force
  Get-InstalledModule
