## 1. Purpose

This policy states the rules for using Flyber systems, accounts, devices, networks, and information. Flyber operates an air-taxi service. Misuse of systems or data can harm riders, crews, aircraft, partners, and the Company. This policy exists so that every person who is given access knows what is required, what is forbidden, and what will happen if they do not comply.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- employees, officers, and directors of Flyber, Inc. and its subsidiaries;
- contractors, consultants, temporary workers, interns, and vendors who are given Flyber accounts, devices, or data (“workforce”);
- Company-owned and Company-managed systems;
- personal devices and personal accounts to the extent they are used to store, process, or access Flyber information.

This policy applies wherever work is performed: offices, vertiports, hangars, aircraft, homes, hotels, and public networks.

## 3. Definitions

**Company systems** means Flyber-owned or Flyber-managed hardware, software, cloud services, email, messaging, identity services, flight-operations tools, payment systems, and networks.

**Flyber information** means data Flyber creates, receives, or holds, classified under POL-SEC-004.

**Public AI tool** means a generative-AI or similar service that is not under a Flyber contract that provides appropriate confidentiality, logging, and data-use terms.

**User** means any person in scope of Section 2.

## 4. Policy statements

### 4.1 Ownership and no expectation of privacy

Company systems and Flyber information are Company property. Users shall have no expectation of privacy when using Company systems, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Flyber may monitor, log, inspect, and preserve activity, devices, and accounts for security, legal, safety, and operational reasons. Personal files or messages placed on Company systems may be reviewed.

### 4.2 Authorized use

Users shall use Company systems and Flyber information only for Flyber business and other uses the Company has expressly allowed. Limited incidental personal use of email and web browsing is permitted if it is lawful, infrequent, does not interfere with work or safety, and does not store personal content that would be damaging if reviewed.

Users shall not use Company systems to operate a side business, to mine cryptocurrency, to run undisclosed software services, or to provide access to any third party who is not an authorized User.

### 4.3 Accounts, credentials, and identity

Users shall:

- use only the account issued to them;
- protect passwords, passkeys, and MFA factors, and not share them;
- not write credentials on paper left at pads, in aircraft, or in shared spaces;
- lock or log off unattended workstations and mobile devices;
- report lost devices, suspected credential theft, or unexpected MFA prompts immediately under POL-SEC-002.

Users shall not:

- request or use another person’s credentials;
- disable MFA, device encryption, or endpoint protection;
- attach an unknown USB or other media to Company systems without CISO approval.

### 4.4 Handling of Flyber information

Users shall handle information according to POL-SEC-004. In particular:

- Rider identity, payment data, precise location, trip history, and aircraft telemetry shall be accessed only to perform assigned duties.
- Users shall not look up a rider, colleague, public figure, or acquaintance “to see where they went,” to satisfy curiosity, or to share a story.
- Users shall not post operational details, pad locations not already Public, incident facts, rider information, or aircraft imagery that reveals non-public operations on social media, messaging apps, or to the press, unless Corporate Communications and Legal have authorized it.
- Restricted and Confidential information shall not be sent to personal email, personal cloud storage, personal messaging apps, or unapproved devices.
- Paper containing Confidential or Restricted information shall be recovered from printers, not left on vertiport counters, and destroyed by approved means.

### 4.5 Artificial intelligence and external tools

Users shall not paste, upload, or dictate Restricted or Confidential Flyber information into a Public AI tool, a consumer translation site, a personal note-taking app, or any vendor system that is not approved for that classification.

Approved Flyber-contracted AI or analytics tools may be used in accordance with their standard and the data-classification rules.

Code, prompts, and documents that contain only Public or Internal information may be used with Public AI tools, provided the user does not include rider data, credentials, keys, network diagrams, unreleased financials, or safety findings.

### 4.6 Safety-critical and operational technology

Users who support flight operations, dispatch, vertiport access control, aircraft telemetry, or maintenance systems shall:

- follow change-control and dual-control rules for those systems;
- not install unofficial software, browser extensions, or remote-access tools on operations workstations;
- not connect personal hotspots or unauthorized network gear to operations or hangar networks;
- treat a suspected compromise of those systems as a safety issue as well as a security issue, and report it immediately.

### 4.7 Communications and representation

Users shall not present personal opinions as Flyber positions. Only designated spokespeople may speak to media, regulators, or the public about incidents, safety, or security.

Phishing, social engineering, and payment-fraud attempts shall be reported, not forwarded casually. Users shall not click through unexpected payment-change, wire, or gift-card requests, including those that appear to come from an executive.

### 4.8 Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited on Company systems or when handling Flyber information:

- unlawful activity of any kind;
- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexually explicit material, except material that Legal or HR must review as part of an investigation;
- unauthorized access, interception, scanning, or exploitation of systems, including Flyber systems, partner systems, and rider devices — including “testing” without a written engagement under CISO authority;
- introduction of malware, or willful circumvention of security controls;
- removal of Company devices or data from approved locations in a manner that violates classification handling rules;
- recording of riders, crews, or non-public pad operations without a documented operational or legal purpose;
- use of Flyber data to stalk, discriminate against, or commercially exploit a person.

### 4.9 Bring-your-own device

Personal phones and laptops may access Flyber email and low-risk collaboration tools only if they meet the endpoint standard (screen lock, OS support, encryption, and remote-wipe capability for Company data). They shall not be used as the system of record for Restricted data. Flight-operations privileged work shall be performed on Company-managed devices.

Flyber may remotely wipe Company data from a personal device. Users who decline that condition shall not use personal devices for Flyber work.

### 4.10 Departing users

Upon notice of termination, resignation, or end of engagement, Users shall return all Company devices, badges, keys, and information, and shall not retain Flyber information on personal devices or accounts. Access shall be revoked under POL-SEC-003. Confidentiality obligations survive departure.

## 5. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |

| **Users** | Comply; complete training; report violations and suspected incidents. |

## 6. Reporting

Users shall report suspected violations of this policy, lost or stolen devices, suspected phishing, and any actual or suspected unauthorized access to Flyber information immediately through the incident channels in POL-SEC-002.

Retaliation against a person who reports in good faith is prohibited. Knowingly false reports may themselves be a violation.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions shall be requested in writing to the CISO, shall name the control, the duration (not to exceed 90 days unless renewed), the compensating control, and the residual risk. Exceptions that concern Restricted data, privileged access, or safety-related systems require CEO concurrence. The CISO shall log all exceptions and report them quarterly to the Board or its designated committee.

Informal “everyone does it” practice is not an exception.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations may result in withdrawal of access, device seizure for investigation, discipline up to and including termination, contract default, and referral to law enforcement. Directors and officers are not exempt.

Where a violation may also be a safety event, Flight Operations and Legal shall be engaged before any narrative is shared outside the investigation team.

## 9. Training and acknowledgment

All Users shall complete security-awareness training at hire and at least annually, and shall acknowledge this policy in writing or by electronic attestation before receiving access, and again after each material revision.

## 10. Review

The CISO shall review this policy at least annually and after any Severity 1 incident. Material changes require Board approval. Administrative updates (owner title, contact path, numbering) may be made by the CEO.
