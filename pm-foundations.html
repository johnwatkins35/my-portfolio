Project management

Project management foundations

This is a project management practice lab. You are the project manager. The sponsor’s note fixes the finish date and the budget cap. If something has to give, scope flexes. Success is still at least one unit in service on that date. The lab state stays in the browser.

Launch the lab Back to projects

What the lab asks

  • Charter: name the sponsor, the finish date, the budget cap, the constraint that may flex, and the smallest outcome that still counts as success.
  • Schedule: set one predecessor chain from sign-off through go-live. Overlap belongs in the risk response, not the baseline.
  • Budget: leave signed hardware and labor in place, hold 10 percent of the cap as contingency, and put the remainder in software.
  • Risk: a late shipment misses the fixed date. Mitigate it by expediting the first unit. Do not add budget, move the date, or approve nights.
