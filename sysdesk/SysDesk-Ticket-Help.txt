SYSDESK — ticket help (step by step)
Simulated lab. Not a real job. Open this beside the queue if you get stuck.

HOW EVERY TICKET WORKS
1. Open the row (click it).
2. If it says Unclaimed — click Claim ticket (MSP / client tickets).
3. Confirm identity (match name, SAM, host, client).
4. Do the required work (Directory, Network, Remote, Shell, or the desk console).
5. Write 40+ characters of notes (person, SAM, host, client, what you checked, exact action, what you refused).
6. Resolve — or Escalate if the ticket says it is out of scope.
7. Optional: Export write-up.

SAM = short Windows login (jlee, pnair, mchenrs). Not the email. Not just the first name.
