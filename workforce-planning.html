Workforce Planning Strategy · 30 September 2026

NovaTech integrated workforce dashboard

Software company. The planning file covers 3,666 employees. Prepared for the Board Compensation Committee.

  • Decision: approve a pay-and-path retention move, sequenced reskilling, and a 90-day integration sprint for three acquired teams.
  • Number: Product Engineering and Data Science attrition at 22 percent, against a 9.5 percent benchmark. AI-skill coverage at 29.7 percent, against a 65 percent target.
  • Cost of waiting: about $44.2 million to replace those exits. 198 of 1,221 people who need reskilling are enrolled.

People Analytics · 1 October 2026

GlobalTech workforce gap analysis

Financial services firm, 18 months into a digital transformation. Six critical segments.

  • Decision: fund Data Engineering and Cybersecurity first.
  • Number: 364 people in the six segments against Year 3 demand of 765, a gap of 401. Data Engineering is 42 today, 70 in Year 1, and 120 by Year 3.
  • Cost of waiting: Data Engineering attrition is 18 percent and the internal mobility pool is 8. AI/ML attrition is 20 percent.

Strategic Planning · 3 October 2026

MedTech three-year workforce plan

Medical device company, 8,000 employees, shifting from hardware to software-enabled products.

  • Decision: hybrid. Reskill 950, hire 320 critical leads, redesign 600 roles.
  • Number: largest shortages are software 635, customer success 324, and cloud 319. Hardware demand falls by 250 seats.
  • Cost of waiting: empty software and security seats delay the product. The funded plan is $23,175,000 over three years. Year 1 is $10,853,750. ROI is 165.8 percent. If benefits fall 30 percent and costs rise 20 percent, ROI is still 55 percent.
