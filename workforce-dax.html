Power BI · DAX

Workforce DAX lab

Practice lab for the DataCamp course Introduction to DAX in Power BI, finished 7 November 2026. The table is the NovaTech skills inventory from the Strategic Workforce Planning specialization. NovaTech is a course scenario. The dashboard, gap analysis, and three-year plan are on the workforce planning page. The measure counts employees who still need reskilling and leaves the company total row out so those people are not counted twice.

Launch the lab Back to projects

What the lab shows

  • With no department filter the measure is 1,221.
  • Sales is 173. The measure runs again for that filter.
  • Including the COMPANY TOTAL row makes a plain sum 2,442. The measure stays 1,221.
  • The same formula is in measure.dax for Power BI. The table is skills_inventory.csv.
