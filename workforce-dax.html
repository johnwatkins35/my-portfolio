Workforce DAX lab

Practice lab for the DataCamp course Introduction to DAX in Power BI, finished 7 November 2026. The table is the NovaTech skills inventory from the Strategic Workforce Planning specialization. NovaTech is a course scenario, not an employer. The measure counts employees who still need reskilling and leaves the company total row out so those people are not counted twice.
